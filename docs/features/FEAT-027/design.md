## Included requirements

### [ZAI:FR-191 canonical record](requirements/FR-191.md)

> An access grant can be withdrawn by a named person, for a stated reason, without destroying the evidence that it existed. `Membership` carries its own provenance (`grantedByPersonId`, `grantReason`, `grantSource`, `expiresAt`) and its own end (`revokedAt`, `revokedByPersonId`, `revokeReason`), and identity exposes `suspendMembership`, `reinstateMembership`, `revokeMembership` and `offboardPerson` — each requiring a reason, each cascading to the dependent RoleBinding rows, each refusing 409 LAST_OWNER rather than leaving a Business with no live owner. A revoked grant is never deleted; a partial unique index excludes it so the same scope can be granted again as a new row. `resolveViewer` additionally denies a grant past its `expiresAt`. Authority is `ownsBusiness` for a BUSINESS-scoped grant and `ownsTenant` for a TENANT-scoped one, which makes the broadest grant in the system administrable for the first time. `Membership` is written only from `src/modules/identity/`, enforced by a preflight ratchet. HR Remove permits live OPERATOR to revoke one explicitly selected scoped grant, retaining last-owner protection; other authority is unchanged.

### [ZAI:FR-192 canonical record](requirements/FR-192.md)

> The scope of a grant is a declared value, never the meaning of a null, and no database side effect may change it. `Membership.scopeType` is TENANT or BUSINESS held by a CHECK against `businessId`; `RoleBinding` takes the same grammar (TENANT, BUSINESS, BRANCH), extending ADR-033 D3 rather than superseding it. `ON DELETE RESTRICT` replaces `ON DELETE SET NULL` on `Membership.businessId`, `Workspace.businessId` and `Project.businessId`, because the null that cascade wrote was read by `buildDomainsByBusiness` as every Business in the Tenant — deleting one Business promoted its members to the whole group and its owners to `ownsTenant`. Tenant ancestry becomes a database invariant via `Business UNIQUE (id, tenantId)` and composite foreign keys; one live grant per person per scope becomes two partial unique indexes; and `Membership.role` defaults to MEMBER instead of OWNER.
