| FR-192 | The scope of a grant is a declared value, never the meaning of a null, and no database side effect may change it. `Membership.scopeType` is TENANT or BUSINESS held by a CHECK against `businessId`; `RoleBinding` takes the same grammar (TENANT, BUSINESS, BRANCH), extending ADR-033 D3 rather than superseding it. `ON DELETE RESTRICT` replaces `ON DELETE SET NULL` on `Membership.businessId`, `Workspace.businessId` and `Project.businessId`, because the null that cascade wrote was read by `buildDomainsByBusiness` as every Business in the Tenant — deleting one Business promoted its members to the whole group and its owners to `ownsTenant`. Tenant ancestry becomes a database invariant via `Business UNIQUE (id, tenantId)` and composite foreign keys; one live grant per person per scope becomes two partial unique indexes; and `Membership.role` defaults to MEMBER instead of OWNER. | ✅ implemented 2026-09-12 — ADR-077 D3/D4/D5; preconditions verified on production 2026-09-12 and re-run at apply time, where they passed. Migration `20260912120000_access_grant_lifecycle.sql` APPLIED on production 2026-09-12 |
