### [ZAI:FR-235 canonical record](requirements/FR-235.md)

> LINE answer grounding from the published knowledge corpus: a LINE OA account carries a publisher-set `knowledgeGrounding` mode — `BUSINESS_KNOWLEDGE` (default, today's behaviour), `GKS_CORPUS` or `GKS_THEN_BUSINESS_KNOWLEDGE`. In a corpus mode a server-owned LINE job's answer reads the Business's published corpus generation through an in-process reader that implements the existing `knowledge.query` port over `queryKnowledgeCorpus` under the scoped runtime knowledge capability, within a configurable budget (2 500 ms, five results, an evidence packet of at most 8 KiB). A timeout or error is `GKS_UNAVAILABLE` and an empty result `NO_EVIDENCE`; either falls back to business knowledge only in `GKS_THEN_BUSINESS_KNOWLEDGE`, and every hop is traced as `EVIDENCE_SELECTED` with its source, reason, budget and retrieval references (citation, source, snapshot and corpus generation). No evidence from any allowed source means the deterministic reply and no model call. The first Business switched is SmartGift, after ADR-075 Phase 3 is deployed.
