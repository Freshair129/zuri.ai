# FR-035 - Business Overview

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Version** | 1.1.0 |
| **Status** | Implemented |
| **Date** | 2026-08-13 |
| **Relates to** | ADR-008, ADR-013, FR-041, HANDOFF-SHELL-V2-CODEX section 5 step 5 |

The selected Business is the operational overview. It renders only that
Business's project health, domain shortcuts, and Business Strategy (Roadmap plus
goal horizons). When no Business is selected the page asks the viewer to choose
one from Home; the former Group consolidation card grid is not an operational
landing surface. Portfolio progress remains available to reporting consumers.
