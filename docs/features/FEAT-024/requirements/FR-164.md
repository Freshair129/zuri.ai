| FR-164 | Suppliers and purchase orders (procurement) — `Supplier`, `PurchaseOrder` and `PurchaseOrderLine`, the buy side of a Business (ADR-066: the legacy ERD's Phase 5 procurement shapes adapted, corrections recorded): a Supplier is Business-scoped with a human `code` unique per Tenant as an attribute (BR-002), contact, tax id, payment terms and lead time as plain columns, ACTIVE until `ARCHIVE` keeps the row (an archived supplier takes no new order, `SUPPLIER_ARCHIVED`); a purchase order has a generated `code` `PO-YYYYMMDD-NNN` unique per Tenant, names an ACTIVE supplier of its own Business (`SUPPLIER_NOT_FOUND` otherwise) and carries lines (never a JSON blob) that may name a non-archived Inventory `Product` of the same Business at the unit cost agreed for this purchase — integer satang in every column, baht with at most two decimals in the API. Total, received value, outstanding value, each line's received and outstanding quantity and the order's `receiptState` (NONE or PARTIAL or COMPLETE) are computed on every read from the lines and their receipt lines, never stored. Status machine DRAFT → SENT (`SEND`), SENT → RECEIVED only by the goods receipt that completes every line (FR-165), SENT → SHORT_CLOSED (`CLOSE`, the short-close with lines outstanding and a reason), DRAFT or SENT → CANCELLED (`CANCEL`, refused once a receipt exists, `PURCHASE_ORDER_HAS_RECEIPTS`); `UPDATE` replaces lines and the supplier only while DRAFT (`PURCHASE_ORDER_LINES_LOCKED`, `PURCHASE_ORDER_SUPPLIER_LOCKED` after SEND), notes and the expected date while open. Compare-and-swap on `version`, one audit row per write, nothing deleted. Reads need Business visibility plus the `procurement` domain (FR-061; the FR-072 404 without it); writes need Business OWNER or `PROCUREMENT_BUYER` (`procurement.po.write`, FR-076 pattern). | 🟠 implemented locally 2026-09-07 — three models (both schemas, additive migration `20260907010000_procurement` shared with FR-165; production SQL **not applied**), the only writers `supplier-service.js` and `purchase-order-service.js`, `GET/POST /api/procurement/suppliers`, `GET/PATCH /api/procurement/suppliers/[id]`, `GET/POST /api/procurement/purchase-orders`, `GET/PATCH /api/procurement/purchase-orders/[id]`, the new `procurement` domain slot with the `/procurement` dashboard and the `/procurement/purchase-orders` console, `PROCUREMENT_BUYER` role, snapshot coverage; one integration suite (AC-164.1–.5), two unit suites, one e2e spec shared with FR-165. Not claimed: purchase requests and approvals, RFQs and quotes, supplier invoices and payables, an order from a LINE chat |
