# ZAI:FR-234

<!-- canonical-row:start -->
```text
| FR-234 | Context Composer: one agent-lane module, not a service, assembles every model prompt of a LINE turn from the server-built AuthContext, MSP packet slices with provenance, knowledge evidence with citation ids, CRM and ERP operational facts and the account's policy. Authorization is checked first and a denial yields an empty packet, never a partial one; CRM and ERP records outrank GKS evidence, which outranks MSP memory, and memory that contradicts a record is dropped with reason `SUPERSEDED_BY_RECORD`; one prompt-wide budget trims by priority and reports every trim; a group thread's slices never cross into another thread. Each model invocation records exactly one `ContextReceipt` — references, a hash and the budget, never content — on `AgentTraceEvent`, and MSP's injection receipt references it. With no evidence and no facts the model is not called and the deterministic reply is sent. A follow-on to FR-171-P2. | ✅ merged #405 2026-09-14 (main 22767b64; not deployed; TASK-ZAI-092, ADR-091 D7, FEAT-037) — `src/modules/agent/context-composer.js` |
```
