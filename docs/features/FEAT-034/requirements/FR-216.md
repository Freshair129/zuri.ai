| FR-216 | Phase card delivery metrics: each phase card on `/control/roadmap` shows its sprint count, its task count with the count per status, its size as the sum of complexity points (`C-1` = 1, `C-2` = 2, `C-3` = 3), its plan window in calendar days from its start and end dates and its effort estimate in hours from the sizing table the programme document keeps; once the phase is done it also shows the elapsed and active time and the tokens measured for its lanes (FR-217, FR-218) with their sources, and at any status the tokens measured so far. A phase, lane or task with no measurement reads as not measured — never zero and never its prediction — and no measured figure feeds plan progress, a gate or a status (ADR-086 D1). Phase, sprint and task cards whose status is done are tinted light green and those in review light orange in both themes, with the status word kept on the card (NFR-008). | ✅ deployed (merged #383, 6630c1df; ADR-086 D1, D2, D6) — `modules/platform-control/program-delivery-metrics.js`, `ProgramRoadmapBoard.jsx`; `tests/unit/program-delivery-metrics.test.js`, `tests/unit/program-roadmap-board-telemetry.test.js`, `tests/e2e/fr216-programme-delivery-telemetry.spec.js` |
