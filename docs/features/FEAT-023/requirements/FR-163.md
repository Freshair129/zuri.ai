| FR-163 | Payments, verification and revenue (commerce) — `Payment`, a PAYMENT or REFUND against a sales order (ADR-065): `code` `PAY-YYYYMMDD-NNN` unique per Tenant, `method` (TRANSFER / CASH / QR / CARD / OTHER), an exact `amountSatang`, an optional `bankReference` that is an attribute unique per Tenant (the legacy duplicate-slip rule kept, the key refused — BR-002, ADR-054 D4), an optional slip that must be a `FileAsset` of the same Business, `paidAt`; PENDING until a verifier confirms (VERIFIED) or rejects it with a reason (REJECTED); only a PENDING payment can be acted on. Recording needs Business OWNER or `SALES_REP`; verifying or rejecting needs Business OWNER or `PAYMENT_VERIFIER` (`commerce.payment.verify`), because verified money is what everything else is counted from: the order's paid, balance due and payment state (UNPAID / PARTIAL / PAID / OVERPAID / REFUNDED — derived, never a column) follow VERIFIED money only; a refund is never verified beyond what was verifiably paid; a payment cannot be recorded on a CANCELLED order, a refund can. `GET /api/commerce/revenue` counts VERIFIED payments net of VERIFIED refunds on the day the money was paid in the Business's calendar (Asia/Bangkok), by origin (CHAT / WALK_IN / ONLINE) and by day, with pending money beside it — the legacy "ROAS from VERIFIED only" kept; no ad attribution until an Ad model exists. Slip OCR is not verification and is not called. Compare-and-swap on `version`, one audit row per write. | 🟠 implemented locally 2026-09-07 — model (both schemas, migration shared with FR-166; production SQL **not applied**), the only writer `payment-service.js`, the read-only `revenue-read-model.js`, `GET/POST /api/commerce/orders/[id]/payments`, `GET/PATCH /api/commerce/payments/[id]`, `GET /api/commerce/revenue`, the `/commerce` dashboard and the payment panel of the orders console, `PAYMENT_VERIFIER` role; one integration suite (AC-163.1–.4), calculators in the unit suite. Not claimed: slip OCR, receipts and invoices, store credit, bank reconciliation |
