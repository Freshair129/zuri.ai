> Payments, verification and revenue (commerce) — `Payment`, a PAYMENT or REFUND against a sales order (ADR-065): `code` `PAY-YYYYMMDD-NNN` unique per Tenant, `method` (TRANSFER / CASH / QR / CARD / OTHER), an exact `amountSatang`, an optional `bankReference` that is an attribute unique per Tenant (the legacy duplicate-slip rule kept, the key refused — BR-002, ADR-054 D4), an optional slip that must be a `FileAsset` of the same Business, `paidAt`; PENDING until a verifier confirms (VERIFIED) or rejects it with a reason (REJECTED); only a PENDING payment can be acted on.
