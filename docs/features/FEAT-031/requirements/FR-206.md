| FR-206 | The catalogue hygiene report names every SKU-bloat finding in a Business, read-only and computed by a pure function over the catalogue and the ledger: `GET /api/inventory/catalog-hygiene?businessId=&dormantDays=` returns findings of kind `NATURE_MISMATCH` (a SKU whose policy disagrees with its master's nature), `LOOKALIKE_SKUS` (live SKUs under one master with the same normalized name, colour, material and variant), `MASTER_WITHOUT_AXES` (a GOOD master with two or more live SKUs and no declared axes), `MASTER_WITHOUT_SKUS`, `DORMANT_SKU` (counted, on-hand zero, no movement within `dormantDays`, 180 by default), `SKU_WITHOUT_IDENTIFIER`, `SERVICE_WITH_STOCK_FIELDS` and `PHASE_OUT_WITH_STOCK`, each with a severity, the rows it names, a Thai message and the action that repairs it, sorted most severe first, with counts by kind and by severity. The `/inventory/hygiene` tab shows the report beside the lifecycle form; the report writes nothing and the repairs are FR-205 actions a person runs. | 🟠 implemented 2026-09-13 (ADR-083 D6; main 52c9881e deployed 2026-09-13) — `hygieneReport` in `inventory-governance.js`, `inventory-hygiene-service.js`, `GET /api/inventory/catalog-hygiene`, `/inventory/hygiene`; no schema; AC-206.1, `tests/unit/inventory-governance.test.js`, `tests/unit/inventory-hygiene-page.test.js` |
