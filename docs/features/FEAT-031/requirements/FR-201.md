| FR-201 | A product master declares its nature once — GOOD or SERVICE — and every SKU under it inherits that nature (BR-038): `ProductMaster.nature` is fixed at creation with a `defaultStockPolicy` (TRACKED or UNTRACKED) for a GOOD; a SKU under a SERVICE master is always `stockPolicy` SERVICE and a request naming any other policy is refused by code (`INVENTORY_NATURE_MISMATCH`), a SKU under a GOOD master is TRACKED or UNTRACKED and never SERVICE, and a service SKU is stored with `safetyStock` 0, `trackingMode` NONE and no replenishment parameters. `GET /api/inventory/products?nature=SERVICE` and `?stockPolicy=` list one class at a time, the stock summary reports `services` as its own count instead of folding services into `untracked`, and the console's SKU form offers no policy at all under a SERVICE master. Existing masters are backfilled by the migration from their own SKUs (all-SERVICE becomes SERVICE); a service left under a GOOD master keeps working and is reported by FR-206 until moved. | 🟠 implemented locally 2026-09-13 (ADR-083 D1) — `ProductMaster.nature` / `defaultStockPolicy`, `natureRule` in `inventory/domain/inventory-governance.js`, the catalogue service, the summary counts, the dashboard form; migration `20260913120000_inventory_sku_governance` APPLIED on production 2026-09-13 (owner-instructed, ADR-057; ledger row written, effect verified: 3 + 6 columns, both tables with forced RLS, 0 masters to backfill) and main 52c9881e deployed; `tests/integration/fr201-inventory-sku-governance.test.js` AC-201.1, `tests/unit/inventory-governance.test.js` |
