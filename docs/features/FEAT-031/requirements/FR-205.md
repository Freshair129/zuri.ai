| FR-205 | A SKU has a lifecycle and a consolidation path, and it cannot leave the catalogue with stock on it (BR-040): `Product.status` is ACTIVE, PHASE_OUT or ARCHIVED, and the versioned product actions are UPDATE, ARCHIVE, PHASE_OUT, REACTIVATE and MERGE. PHASE_OUT refuses every receipt (`INVENTORY_PRODUCT_PHASED_OUT`) while issues, corrections and the sell-down continue and replenishment never suggests it; ARCHIVE is refused while a counted SKU has on-hand (`INVENTORY_PRODUCT_HAS_STOCK`) or a live reservation (`INVENTORY_PRODUCT_HAS_RESERVATIONS`); REACTIVATE returns a phased-out or archived SKU to ACTIVE (a merged duplicate never: `INVENTORY_PRODUCT_MERGED`); MERGE `{ into }` requires the same Business and nature and an ACTIVE survivor, moves the duplicate's on-hand to the survivor as an ISSUE / RECEIPT pair with reference `MERGE:<code>` when both are TRACKED / NONE (otherwise `INVENTORY_MERGE_REQUIRES_EMPTY_STOCK`), re-points active identifiers and unit conversions, bundle items and recipe lines and recipes to the survivor unless the survivor already appears there or an open work order names the duplicate (`INVENTORY_MERGE_BLOCKED_BY_REFERENCES` with the list), and ends the duplicate ARCHIVED with `mergedIntoProductId` set. Nothing is deleted and no ledger row is rewritten; sales and purchase order lines keep naming the duplicate as history. Every action records the caller's `reason` in its audit row. | 🟠 implemented locally 2026-09-13 (ADR-083 D5) — `productLifecycleRule` / `archiveGuard` / `mergeRule`, `applyProductAction` in `inventory-catalog-service.js`, `movementRule`, `Product.mergedIntoProductId`, the `/inventory/hygiene` merge desk; migration `20260913120000_inventory_sku_governance` APPLIED on production 2026-09-13 (owner-instructed, ADR-057; ledger row written, effect verified: 3 + 6 columns, both tables with forced RLS, 0 masters to backfill) and main 52c9881e deployed; AC-205.1, AC-205.2 |
