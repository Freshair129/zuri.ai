### [ZAI:FR-201 canonical record](requirements/FR-201.md)

> A product master declares its nature once — GOOD or SERVICE — and every SKU under it inherits that nature (BR-038): `ProductMaster.nature` is fixed at creation with a `defaultStockPolicy` (TRACKED or UNTRACKED) for a GOOD; a SKU under a SERVICE master is always `stockPolicy` SERVICE and a request naming any other policy is refused by code (`INVENTORY_NATURE_MISMATCH`), a SKU under a GOOD master is TRACKED or UNTRACKED and never SERVICE, and a service SKU is stored with `safetyStock` 0, `trackingMode` NONE and no replenishment parameters. `GET /api/inventory/products?nature=SERVICE` and `?stockPolicy=` list one class at a time, the stock summary reports `services` as its own count instead of folding services into `untracked`, and the console's SKU form offers no policy at all under a SERVICE master. Existing masters are backfilled by the migration from their own SKUs (all-SERVICE becomes SERVICE); a service left under a GOOD master keeps working and is reported by FR-206 until moved.

### [ZAI:FR-202 canonical record](requirements/FR-202.md)

> A SKU is one combination of its master's variant axes, and the same combination cannot exist twice (BR-039): `ProductMaster.variantAxes` is the ordered list of axes that distinguish its SKUs (`["color","size"]`, fixed at creation), each SKU carries `variant` (one value per declared axis; a missing axis is `INVENTORY_VARIANT_AXES_INCOMPLETE`, an undeclared one `INVENTORY_VARIANT_AXIS_UNKNOWN`), and the service derives `variantKey` — the normalized `axis=value|axis=value` fingerprint — unique per master by index, archived or not, so a second SKU for the same combination is refused with `INVENTORY_PRODUCT_VARIANT_EXISTS` naming the first (and REACTIVATE is the answer when the first is archived). The legacy `color` / `material` columns feed a same-named axis when `variant` is silent. A master that declares no axes has no key and enforces nothing (reported as `MASTER_WITHOUT_AXES`); for every master the exact-match lookalike guard refuses a new SKU whose normalized name, colour, material and variant match a live SKU under the same master (`INVENTORY_PRODUCT_LOOKALIKE`) unless the caller passes `allowLookalike`, which the audit row records. `UPDATE` may correct a variant; it is re-keyed and re-checked.

### [ZAI:FR-204 canonical record](requirements/FR-204.md)

> A pack size is a unit conversion on the SKU, never a second SKU, and the ledger counts base units only (BR-037): `ProductUnitConversion` declares per SKU a `unit` (`BOX12`, `CTN`), an integer `factor` (base units per one of that unit), a `usage` (PURCHASE / SALES / ANY) and ACTIVE / RETIRED, unique per `(productId, unit)`; the base unit itself is refused (`INVENTORY_UNIT_IS_BASE`), a serial-tracked product takes none (`INVENTORY_UNIT_NOT_FOR_SERIAL`, one serial is one unit) and a service has none. ### [ZAI:FR-205 canonical record](requirements/FR-205.md)

> A SKU has a lifecycle and a consolidation path, and it cannot leave the catalogue with stock on it (BR-040): `Product.status` is ACTIVE, PHASE_OUT or ARCHIVED, and the versioned product actions are UPDATE, ARCHIVE, PHASE_OUT, REACTIVATE and MERGE. PHASE_OUT refuses every receipt (`INVENTORY_PRODUCT_PHASED_OUT`) while issues, corrections and the sell-down continue and replenishment never suggests it; ARCHIVE is refused while a counted SKU has on-hand (`INVENTORY_PRODUCT_HAS_STOCK`) or a live reservation (`INVENTORY_PRODUCT_HAS_RESERVATIONS`); REACTIVATE returns a phased-out or archived SKU to ACTIVE (a merged duplicate never: `INVENTORY_PRODUCT_MERGED`); MERGE `{ into }` requires the same Business and nature and an ACTIVE survivor, moves the duplicate's on-hand to the survivor as an ISSUE / RECEIPT pair with reference `MERGE:<code>` when both are TRACKED / NONE (otherwise `INVENTORY_MERGE_REQUIRES_EMPTY_STOCK`), re-points active identifiers and unit conversions, bundle items and recipe lines and recipes to the survivor unless the survivor already appears there or an open work order names the duplicate (`INVENTORY_MERGE_BLOCKED_BY_REFERENCES` with the list), and ends the duplicate ARCHIVED with `mergedIntoProductId` set. Nothing is deleted and no ledger row is rewritten; sales and purchase order lines keep naming the duplicate as history. Every action records the caller's `reason` in its audit row.
