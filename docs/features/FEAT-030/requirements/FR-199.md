```text
| FR-199 | A Business owner can read the access history of their own scope. `listAccessHistory({ businessId / tenantId / personId })` returns events in the MEMBERSHIP, ROLE_BINDING, ACCESS_INVITE and access-related PERSON families for exactly one scope, newest first, each with its `before`/`after` snapshot and the actor joined to `{ id, code, displayName }`; authority is `ownsBusiness`, `ownsTenant`, oneself, or the installation operator, refusing 404-shaped identically for a scope the caller does not own and one that does not exist (SEC-001). `listBusinessAccess({ businessId })` returns the current-state roster — every grant in that Business in every status, with `grantedBy`/`grantReason`/`grantSource`/`revokedBy`/`revokeReason` — the "who has access right now, and who gave it to them" table no surface produced before this. Exposed as `GET /api/platform/access-history` and `GET /api/platform/businesses/[businessId]/grants`, with a read-only panel on the platform users page. | ✅ implemented 2026-09-12 — ADR-080 D3/D4; closes the read-side gap ADR-077's whole lifecycle design assumed was already possible |
```
