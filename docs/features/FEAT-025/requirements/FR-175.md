| FR-175 | Inventory valuation is landed cost in integer satang, and domestic single-drop freight is absorbed into it rather than quoted — `StockMovement.costSatang` is the unit landed cost of that row (THB × 100, never a float, the ADR-065 D2 money rule). `landedUnitCostSatang = factoryCostSatang + ceil(seaFreight/batch) + ceil(duty/batch) + ceil(inboundTruck/batch) + customizationPerUnit + kittingPerUnit`, each shared cost divided by the batch and rounded up to the satang so a batch is never valued below what it cost. The inbound truck defaults to the flat 250,000 satang (2,500 THB) single-drop rate, which is why a standard quote shows delivery as 0.00 and must carry no separate freight line — a second drop or an island crossing is a different service and belongs to Commerce. Valuation across receipts is moving weighted average, recomputed from the ledger like every other inventory number. | 🟠 implemented locally 2026-09-10 (ADR-074 D3) — `inventory/domain/inventory-costing.js` (pure), `costSatang` on the ledger; `tests/unit/inventory-costing.test.js`, `tests/integration/fr177-kitting-work-order.test.js` |
