```text
| FR-179 | A stored unit that ages and can be restored by an operation is guarded on issue, not merely reported — `Product.maintenanceIntervalDays` and `Product.maxStorageDays` (both nullable, so a product with neither behaves exactly as before) and `ProductLot.lastMaintainedAt` make a lot's storage age `now − (lastMaintainedAt ?? manufacturedAt)`. At the maintenance interval the lot is **due**: surfaced as a task, never blocked. Past `maxStorageDays` it is **refused** for issue and for kitting until a maintenance is recorded, which resets the clock. The generalisation past "battery" is deliberate — the rule is about a unit that degrades in storage and is restored by an operation, which is as true of a lithium cell needing a 180-day recharge as of a calibrated instrument. FEFO (FR-155) still chooses which lot goes first; this says which lots may go at all. | 🟠 implemented locally 2026-09-10 (ADR-074 D7, BR-030) — two columns + `lastMaintainedAt`, `inventory/domain/inventory-wip.js` shelf-life calculators, `application/inventory-shelf-life-service.js`; `tests/integration/fr179-shelf-life-guard.test.js` |
```
