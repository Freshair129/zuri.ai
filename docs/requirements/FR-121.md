# ZAI:FR-121

```text
| FR-121 | Google as a second way in, over the same `Person`: a visitor may present a Google account at `/signup` and `/login`, and the OAuth callback resolves the provider `sub` to exactly one internal `Person` through a namespaced external binding (FR-094) — a second way to prove who you are, never a second account path. Sign-in follows sign-up necessarily: a person who signed up with Google set no password, so refusing Google at `/login` would strand the account behind a credential that was never created. The account it produces grants nothing, exactly as FR-120: no `PlatformGrant`, Tenant, Business, Space, Project or `WorkspaceMembership`. **It does not satisfy FR-122** — Google supplies an address and a display name of its own choosing, and neither is the given name, family name or telephone number a Profile requires; the telephone number Google never supplies at all. An address matching an existing local account binds to that `Person` **only when Google asserts `email_verified`**; an unverified assertion is refused rather than linked, because linking on an unverified address is account takeover performed by signing up. **Declared blocked, not merely unbuilt**, and on two named things rather than on effort: this installation holds no OAuth client credential, and `ExternalIdentity` is keyed `(tenantId, provider, providerSubject)` with `tenantId` a required foreign key — which a self-serve signup deliberately does not have (FR-120 creates no Tenant), so the binding needs a tenant-less form before any of this can be written. | 🔜 declared only — blocked on an OAuth client credential this installation does not hold, and on `ExternalIdentity` requiring a Tenant a self-serve signup has none of |
```
