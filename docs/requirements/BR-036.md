| BR-036 | An audit event states its scope as data a query can filter on, never only as a fact buried inside a free-form payload. A mutation that touches a Tenant or a Business records that Tenant or Business id on the row; a mutation with a reason records the reason on the row. Adding this capability may never require rewriting a row that already exists — an old row that predates the capability states its scope as absent, never as an inferred guess standing in for evidence nobody wrote. | FR-198, FR-199; ADR-080 D1; SEC-003 |
