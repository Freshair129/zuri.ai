```text
| BR-027 | Money in the stock ledger is integer satang and domestic single-drop freight is absorbed into unit valuation, never quoted as a line — `costSatang` on a movement is the unit landed cost in THB × 100; a float never enters. Shared batch costs (sea freight, duty, the flat inbound truck) are divided by the batch quantity and rounded **up** to the satang, so a batch is never valued below what it cost and the rounding gain is never redistributed, because a valuation that depends on iteration order is not reproducible. A standard single-drop delivery consequently appears to the customer as 0.00; a second drop or an island crossing is a distinct service and is charged by the Commerce lane, not by re-opening the valuation. | FR-175; ADR-074 D3; ADR-065 D2 |
```
