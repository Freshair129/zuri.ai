| NFR-023 | Person-attributed usage retention is bounded: raw `UsageEvent` rows carrying a `personId` are retained 90 days, matching this codebase's existing default for person-adjacent content (`RawExternalRecord` payload, MSP session content, `AgentTraceEvent` payload); after 90 days a daily `(date, route or actionName, count)` rollup replaces them with no `personId`. `ErrorEvent` carries no `personId`. Both `ErrorEvent` and `UsageEvent` are operator-only reads with no consent gate — internal operational records of the system's own signed-in users, not external customer data — and the operator-facing view states what is collected and for how long (ADR-095 D3, D4). | 🟠 implemented locally 2026-09-16 (ADR-095 D3, D4) — `rollupUsageEvents()` (`application/usage-events.js`), `POST /api/platform/usage-events/rollup` (`ZURI_USAGE_ROLLUP_TOKEN`, deployment-authenticated); `tests/unit/usage-events.test.js`; not merged, rollup route not yet scheduled |
