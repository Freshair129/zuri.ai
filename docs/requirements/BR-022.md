| BR-022 | A knowledge object that fails a stage is quarantined with its full failure envelope — `job_id`, `artifact_id`, `stage`, `error_code`, `error_message`, `retry_count`, `first_failed_at`, `last_failed_at`, `pipeline_version` (spec §28) — and classified as retryable, non-retryable or review-required (spec §27). Silent loss is the one outcome forbidden: a corpus that quietly omits what it could not parse reports a completeness it does not have, and every downstream answer inherits that overstatement without a way to detect it. |
