| BR-030 | A unit that degrades in storage is guarded on issue, not merely reported — for a product declaring `maintenanceIntervalDays` and/or `maxStorageDays`, a lot's storage age runs from `lastMaintainedAt ?? manufacturedAt`. At the maintenance interval the lot is due and is surfaced as a task while remaining fully issuable; past `maxStorageDays` it is refused for issue and for kitting until a maintenance is recorded, which resets the clock. A product declaring neither is never affected. This is FEFO's second question — FR-155's FEFO decides which lot goes first, this decides which lots may go at all. | FR-179; ADR-074 D7 |
