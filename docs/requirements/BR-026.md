```text
| BR-026 | Stock changes place only through a transfer, and a transfer is one movement pair in one transaction — the issue at the source and the receipt at the target are written together or not at all, both through the FR-155 ledger writer, so a Business-wide on-hand is unchanged by construction while the located buckets move. A movement may name a source, a target, both (a transfer) or neither (a row written before FR-174, or one whose location is genuinely unknown); a located sum is therefore always reported as its own figure beside the Business-wide total, never as a replacement for it. | FR-174; ADR-074 D1, D2 |
```
