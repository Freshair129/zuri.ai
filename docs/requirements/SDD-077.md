# ZAI:SDD-077

```text
| SDD-077 | A second `knowledge_type` in `business_knowledge` needs its own registered query and its own contract version, because every existing query filters on no type at all. `registeredPredicate` (`src/modules/knowledge/postgres-business-knowledge.js`) builds three predicates — `product_detail`, `product_compare`, and `product_search`'s `ilike` across `product_code`, `name`, `category` and `description` — and the SQL around them constrains `tenant_id`, `business_id`, `sensitivity = 'PUBLIC'` and `is_active`, and nothing else. Today that is safe by accident rather than by design: `knowledge_type` is check-constrained to `'PRODUCT'` in the DDL and `z.literal('PRODUCT')` in `business-contract.js`, and one kind cannot be mistaken for another while there is only one kind. The moment FR-131 widens that constraint, those same three queries answer product questions with rate rows and `product_compare` on two rate codes returns them as products — a wrong answer delivered on the LINE surface with nothing anywhere reporting a problem. So the widening is one decision in four places, not one: the DDL check, the Zod literal, a `contract_version` that is no longer `'1.0.0'`, and a fourth registered query that names the type it serves. Holding rates in a new `ShippingRateMatrix` Prisma model instead, as CR-005 proposes, was rejected on this repository's own terms rather than on taste: it puts one Business's published prices in two stores with two provenance stories — one with `as_of`, `approved_at`, `source_sha256` and an FR-129 signature, one with `updatedAt` — and the second cannot say what a past quotation was priced from. This is deliberately **not** SDD-062 and points the other way. That decision is about `sensitivity`, and says widening what knowledge may CARRY must not widen what is SERVED. Widening `knowledge_type` must widen what is served, because the agent has to be able to quote a rate, so the containment here is a typed query rather than an unchanged filter — and reading SDD-062 as already covering this case is the specific mistake this decision exists to stop. | FR-131; FR-132, FR-047, FR-129, SDD-025, SDD-026, SDD-062, SDD-075 |
```
