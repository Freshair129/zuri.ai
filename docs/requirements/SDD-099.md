| SDD-099 | The LINE grounding reader is a knowledge-lane implementation of the existing `knowledge.query` port that calls `queryKnowledgeCorpus` in process — no HTTP self-call and no Tier 4 read — with the job's server-derived Tenant and Business under the ADR-072 D5 runtime capability; it maps corpus results to the evidence records `verifyCandidate` already checks, enforces `ZURI_LINE_KNOWLEDGE_BUDGET_MS` (default 2 500), a top-K of 5 and an 8 KiB packet cap as configuration, and composes with the business-knowledge reader in the fixed order of the account's grounding mode, each hop wrapped by the existing `tracedKnowledge`. `answerBusinessQuestion` and `withLineCatalogCommand` are unchanged. | FR-235; SEC-032; ADR-090 D1–D4 |
