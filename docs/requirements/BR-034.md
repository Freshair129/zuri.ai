| BR-034 | An HR assignment record and an access grant are two different facts and neither derives the other. Ending an Employment writes no change to any Membership row, and revoking a Membership writes no change to any Employment row; an owner who wants both performs both acts, each separately audited. A LegalEntity is owned by exactly one Tenant, and a Business may reference a LegalEntity only within its own Tenant — no ancestor's deletion or update may cross that boundary. A VAT branch code belongs to the legal entity's own tax registration, never to an operating site; a site may exist, and issue every document type that does not require one, with no tax registration linked at all. | FR-193, FR-194; ADR-078 D1, D2 |
