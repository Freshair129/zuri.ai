| FR-131 | Shipping rate card as governed business knowledge: a Business's logistics rate matrix — Member tier × warehouse × shipping method × category, each cell a THB/CBM rate and a THB/kg rate with the CBM and weight minima that qualify a shipment for that tier — is published as `business_knowledge` rows under a **second `knowledge_type`**, and declares **no Prisma model, no new domain and no settings write path**. (a) The rows go where the same Business's product prices already go. `zuri_core.business_knowledge` is Tenant- and Business-scoped under forced RLS behind a `SET LOCAL ROLE` login (SDD-026) and already carries every fact a rate needs: `as_of` for the sheet's effective date, `approved_at`, `source_ref` and `source_sha256` for the supplier rate sheet the numbers came from, `is_active` for supersession, plus `unit`, `sell_price` and a `specification` object for the per-kilogram rate and the tier minima. CR-005's `ShippingRateMatrix` Prisma model is refused for the reason SDD-077 gives, and additionally because no chartered domain may hold it: `knowledge` owns no Prisma models by design and has kept that boundary through FR-109…FR-119, and `market-intelligence` reserves commercial state for a "Commerce authority" that has no charter in this repository. (b) Rates enter through the intake already built for this exact input. The sheets are supplier documents, so `SMARTGIFT_DOCUMENT_INTAKE` stages them (`document-intake-contract.js` accepts image sources), `DPL-SUPABASE-BUSINESS-KNOWLEDGE-V1` publishes them, and FR-129's `APPROVED` `PipelineGateDecision` is what makes a rate change readable. The administrator-editable grid of CR-005 §3.B.1 is refused on that ground rather than on effort: a rate a person may silently retype has no effective date, no signature and no source, and a quotation priced from it cannot be explained afterwards. (c) The density switch — charge by weight at or above 400 kg/CBM, by volume below — is a property of the published card, not of a cell, and is carried once per version rather than copied onto every cell, where sixty-four copies are sixty-four chances to disagree. (d) **Sell-side only.** FR-047 excludes cost and margin from this projection by name; what a customer is charged belongs here and the cost basis behind CR-005 §4.1's margin floor does not (FR-132 (c)). (e) Scope is `(tenant_id, business_id)` and not the `workspaceId` CR-005 proposes: `Workspace.tenantId` and `Workspace.businessId` are both nullable under `scopeType` `PORTFOLIO`/`TENANT`/`BUSINESS`, and a LINE turn resolves `{tenantId, businessId}` from its server-owned binding (FR-052) and never holds a `workspaceId` at all, so a workspace-keyed rate is unreadable from the one surface that needs it. Reworked from CR-005; the quoting behaviour is FR-132 | 🔜 declared only — blocked on three named things and one unmade decision. The store pins `knowledge_type = 'PRODUCT'` and `contract_version = '1.0.0'` as DDL check constraints and `business-contract.js` mirrors both as `z.literal`, so a second kind cannot be stored until all four are widened together; `registeredPredicate` in `postgres-business-knowledge.js` constrains `tenant_id`, `business_id`, `sensitivity` and `is_active` and no type at all, so widening the constraint before SDD-077's typed query lands would make `product_search` and `product_compare` answer product questions with rate rows; and nobody has decided whether a supplier's rate sheet is the Business's own knowledge or needs a per-supplier scope this table has no column for |
