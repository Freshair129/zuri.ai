| SDD-078 | Physical Asset identity is separate from content identity and external-document identity. `RegisteredAsset.id` is the internal UUID, `assetCode` is the stable Business-scoped human ID, `FileAsset.id` is referenced by `AssetEvidence`, and PR/PO/payment/serial/lot values remain typed attributes or references. No one value is reused as another concept's key. | FR-133..135; BR-002; ADR-055 |
