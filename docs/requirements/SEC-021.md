# ZAI:SEC-021

<!-- canonical-row:start -->
```text
| SEC-021 | Knowledge ingestion never deduplicates across tenants and never embeds credentials or access tokens: deduplication compares only within one tenant's scope, because two tenants holding a content-identical document hold two facts, and collapsing them is a cross-tenant disclosure wearing the costume of an optimisation (spec §34; SEC-001). Secrets are redacted before parsing and are never embedded, never indexed, and never written into a log line or a quarantine record — a quarantine store is exactly where an unredacted failure payload would come to rest. Source authenticity is verified wherever the channel permits it; transport and sensitive storage are encrypted. | ✅ implemented 2026-08-28 by FR-117 — the pipeline it was declared ahead of |
```
