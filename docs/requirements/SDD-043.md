| SDD-043 | The Phase 1 runtime is composed in two stages: resolve the server-owned LINE binding and trusted Tenant/Business scope, then select one database-enforced active primary IntegrationConnection for `PHASE1_LINE_LLM` and resolve its opaque secret through an explicit runtime-source SecretManagerPort before constructing ModelProviderPort.
