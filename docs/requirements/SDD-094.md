# ZAI:SDD-094

```text
| SDD-094 | One generalised invitation table, not three parallel ones: `AccessInvite` (renamed from `WorkspaceInvite`) carries `scopeType` (PORTFOLIO / TENANT / BUSINESS) with a CHECK binding it to exactly one of `portfolioId`/`tenantId`/`businessId`, and an addressee CHECK requiring at least one of `targetPersonId`/`invitedEmail`/`invitedLineUserId`. `RoleBinding.businessId` becomes nullable to admit the TENANT scope ADR-077 D3 declared and left unresolved; `resolveViewer` expands a TENANT binding to every ACTIVE Business the Tenant holds at each request — including Businesses created after the grant, which a TENANT binding reaches without a new assignment, independent of `visibleBusinessIds`, and `hasPermission` is unchanged. `PlatformGrant` drops its unconditional `(personId, capability)` unique in favour of one scoped to `status = 'ACTIVE'`, so a superseded or expired grant survives as history rather than blocking a renewal. | FR-192, FR-195, FR-197; ADR-079 |
```
