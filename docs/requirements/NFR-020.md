# ZAI:NFR-020

<!-- canonical-row:start -->
```text
| NFR-020 | Every knowledge ingestion stage emits per-stage and pipeline-level metrics: each stage reports `records_in`, `records_out`, `records_failed`, `records_quarantined`, `processing_time` and `retry_count`; the pipeline reports `ingestion_lag`, `pipeline_latency`, `publication_latency`, `error_rate`, `quarantine_rate`, `entity_resolution_rate` and `retrieval_quality_score` (spec §32). A stage that reports nothing is indistinguishable from a stage that did nothing — that is the failure mode this requires away, and it is why the counts are per stage rather than per run. | 🟠 partial overall. The legacy ledger computes four metrics; ADR-073 GenesisRAG17StageEvidence additionally persists all six frozen wire counters: records_in, records_out, records_quarantined, error_count, retry_count, duration_ms with actual operation timestamps. The wire names error_count/duration_ms are not a rename of this requirement's records_failed/processing_time, and this isolated proof does not establish every product-wide pipeline aggregate listed here. See the [wire contract](plans/GENESISRAG17-CONTRACT.md) and acceptance report for measured evidence; earlier two-unwired-counter limitation describes the legacy slice only. |
```
