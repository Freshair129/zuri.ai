| SEC-026 | Erasure is downstream of offboarding and never a substitute for it: `erasePrincipal` refuses 409 PRINCIPAL_HAS_LIVE_GRANTS while the principal holds any non-revoked Membership, RoleBinding or PlatformGrant in the tenant being erased, and where it does proceed it deletes `PersonCredential` and sets `Person.accessDisabledAt`, because a redacted person whose password still works is not erased. Erasure never revokes grants itself — that would make a data-subject request an administrative act with no named author and bury the moment authority ended inside a deliberately redacted trail. | 🔜 FR-191 / ADR-077 D6 — declared 2026-09-12; replaces the prior behaviour where erasure counted memberships to decide whether to redact the Person and ended none |
