| BR-035 | Two people complete a cycle that touches money or stock; one person never does, however senior. A role that both records and confirms the same class of fact (recording a payment and verifying it; writing a purchase order and receiving against it) is a segregation-of-duties conflict, refused at assignment unless a Tenant owner overrides it with a recorded reason, and refused again at the transaction itself when the SAME person would record and confirm the SAME instance — a rule an OWNER does not bypass, because it is about needing a second person, not about holding a permission. An exemption for a genuinely one-person Business must be an explicit, audited attestation, never a silent one. | FR-196; ADR-065 D4; ADR-066 D4 as amended by ADR-079 |
