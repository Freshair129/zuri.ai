```text
| BR-018 | Team membership is never an input to an authorization decision. A Team groups people; it does not grant, widen or imply any scope, role or domain visibility, and the identity resolver never reads it. Stated as a rule rather than left as an intention because a negative invariant holds only while something checks it — `Membership` is the authority record, and merging a grouping into it is what let a single unauthenticated POST mint business-owner authority on 2026-08-17. |
```
