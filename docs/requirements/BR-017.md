| BR-017 | There is no bare edge. A dependency edge cannot be created without a declared Handoff Contract, and a declared-but-unsatisfied contract does not release its successor. This is a *domain* contract — the obligation between two pieces of work — and is deliberately not the intake envelope of BR-009, which is a *transport* contract governing the shape of a payload crossing the system boundary. The two keep separate names because merging them would put schema validation and project governance in one bucket, leaving the product with one word for two different kinds of "invalid". |
