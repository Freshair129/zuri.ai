| SDD-040 | Identity binding is explicit at every axis: product domain catalog IDs map to route keys/labels, technical owner IDs map to charters, `executionModeId` resolves the seven mode contracts, `planId` aliases the committed Workstream UUID, `goal_id` aliases `BusinessGoal.id` through `ProjectGoal`, `risk_id` aliases an authorized Project Manager Risk record when available, every mode-specific period alias (`phaseId`, `sprintId`, `stageId`, `batchId`, `waveId`, `periodId`, etc.) aliases `WorkContainer.id`, every item alias aliases `WorkItem.id`, and WorkItem tag attachments use `tagId`. Supporting owner-resolved references are explicit: `node_id`, `edge_id`, `artifact_id`, `contract_id` (CRM Contact), `meeting_id`, `call_id`, `followup_id`, `req_id`, `verify_id`, `gate_id`, `integration_id`, `graph_id`, `workflow_contract_id`, `workflow_id`, `runbook_id`, `promotion_id`, `skill_id` and `tool_id`. `contract_id` is CRM Contact context, `execution_contract_id` is the seven-mode Execution contract, and `workflow_contract_id` is the multi-agent workflow contract. `req_id` resolves to a declared requirement key; `runbook_id` is the concrete procedure under `workflow_id`; and `promotion_id` is the governed knowledge-promotion occurrence. `B2B_SALES` binds to Commerce primary + CRM supporting; `B2C_CAMPAIGN` to Marketing primary + CRM supporting; `OPERATIONS` to Operations primary; all seven execution records remain technically owned by Project Manager. Legacy labels are normalized before authorization, unresolved IDs fail closed, and the same IDs appear in Roadmap, specialized views, Agent import and AuditEvent. | FR-070; ADR-029, FR-069, SDD-039, BR-004, BR-007, BR-009, SEC-002, SEC-003, SEC-008 |
