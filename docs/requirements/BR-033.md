| BR-033 | A grant is withdrawn only by an explicit act that records who withdrew it and why. No referential action, cascade or other database side effect may change the scope of a grant, and none may widen one: a parent row whose deletion would reshape a grant must be refused until the grant is withdrawn. A status vocabulary that a resolver branches on must have a service that writes each of its values — a read filter over a column nothing writes is not an implemented control. | FR-191, FR-192; ADR-077 D2/D4/D7; `.brain/rca/2026-09-12-a-grant-that-cannot-be-withdrawn.md` |
