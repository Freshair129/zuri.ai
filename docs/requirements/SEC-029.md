| SEC-029 | A TOTP factor secret is never stored or backed up in the clear, and a stored value cannot be moved or forged into a working factor: `MfaFactor.secret` holds only an authenticated ciphertext bound to its Person and factor row, under a key the database does not hold. Sealing fails closed — a production deployment without a valid key answers 503 `MFA_SECRET_KEY_REQUIRED` before any factor is read, written or deleted — and opening refuses a legacy plaintext value, an unavailable key version, a value bound to another row and any tampering, so such a factor cannot verify a code. No secret, sealed or opened, appears in any response other than the one-time enrollment reply, nor in an audit payload or a log line. | 🟠 implemented 2026-09-14 on fix/identity-mfa-secret-at-rest — ADR-088; key provisioning and the reseal sweep are operator steps (ADR-057), not performed |
