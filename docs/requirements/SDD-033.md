```text
| SDD-033 | Business Home is a **non-owning cross-domain projection** (architecture invariant 8): it lives in the `business` read slice, owns no model, writes nothing, and calls each domain's read model rather than querying another domain's tables. A figure it cannot source from a live domain is not rendered — reserved domains show as reserved, and any composite score states which domains it covers. This keeps the surface from becoming a second, drifting copy of numbers the domains already own. | FR-060; ADR-025, BR-004, NFR-006 |
```
