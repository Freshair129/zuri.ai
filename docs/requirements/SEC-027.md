```text
| SEC-027 | Installation-operator access is time-boxed and its use is auditable, not only its grant. Every non-bootstrap `PlatformGrant` carries a mandatory `expiresAt` capped at 90 days, honoured by `hasOperatorGrant` even while the row is still nominally ACTIVE; renewal is a fresh row so the history of who held operator access, and when, is never overwritten. Reading the installation-wide audit stream and previewing or restoring a whole-installation backup are support-class reads (ADR-017 D6) and each records one `OPERATOR_ACTION` audit event on use — a denied attempt records nothing. | FR-197; ADR-017 D6 as extended by ADR-079 |
```
