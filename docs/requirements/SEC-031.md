| SEC-031 | Every copy of a person's conversation content has a retention window and an erasure path: the CRM record, raw LINE evidence, execution trace inputs, MSP session events, episodic and passport memory, knowledge candidates and the chat evidence archive (FR-245) are each tombstoned or destroyed by retention or erasure; a Tenant may shorten a window but never lengthen it; and a tier outside Tier 1 counts as erased only once it has acknowledged the erasure, the pending state staying visible until then and never silently dropped. A PDPA erasure may be deferred only for the chat evidence archive and only while an OWNER-recorded legal hold on that Customer is active (SEC-034); the hold is visible on the erasure status and never extends to any other copy. MSP session memory requires the account's memory policy; episodic, passport and cross-thread memory additionally require the Customer's consent GRANTED and a DIRECT audience. | 🔜 declared only 2026-09-14 — FR-230..FR-232 / ADR-091 D2, D4, D6 security gate |
