# ZAI:BR-044

```text
| BR-044 | A `BusinessGoal`'s `progress` is never both hand-typed and derived from Key Results at once. The moment a goal holds its first non-archived `BusinessKeyResult`, a manual `progress` patch is refused (`updateGoal`), and every reader of that goal's progress — the strategy card, the per-project roadmap projection, the marketing Strategy Workspace — agrees, because there is exactly one writer of the number. | FR-268; SDD-107; ADR-101 |
```
