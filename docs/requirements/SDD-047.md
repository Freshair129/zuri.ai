```text
| SDD-047 | Projects Overview is one composed read model behind one route (`GET /api/projects/overview`): the KPI counts and the list rows, progress included, in a single response. A progress bar per row would otherwise mean one `/api/progress/project/{id}` call per project — an N+1 across the page's main content. It follows SDD-045's discipline: authorize the scope before composing, read through the pure calculators, never mutate `progressCache` on a read, and leave the existing `/api/projects` list contract untouched so its consumers do not move (the cost of moving it is recorded in `.brain/rca/2026-08-18-project-list-envelope-broke-relation-consumers.md`). | FR-086; ADR-036, FR-003, FR-077, SDD-045, ADR-034 |
```
