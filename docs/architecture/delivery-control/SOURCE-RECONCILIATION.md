## Source and ownership limits

The repository source-of-truth profile preserves exact canonical rows, issued
IDs, original ADR lifecycle, and historical snapshots. The canonical-format
contract requires reviewed record migration for changed source-row meaning;
readers and exports are generator-owned. Therefore this packet uses source
dispositions and navigation pointers only. It does not edit a canonical row,
FR/FEAT registry, roadmap export, generated reader, or historical receipt.

Root owns future delivery-control tools under `tools/delivery-control/`; local
non-product state is reserved under `<git-common-dir>/zuri-delivery-control/`.
Schema/backend selection belongs to separately admitted WF02 work. No
sixteenth business domain, domain owner, or Business permission is introduced.
