### Packet contract and use

An operator-owned JSON packet has `schemaVersion: 1`; `inputs` entries contain `path`, raw-byte `sha256` and `role` (`SEMANTIC_INPUT` or `REVIEW_CONTEXT`). `readSetSha256` is SHA-256 of UTF-8 `JSON.stringify(inputs)` in the supplied order. Candidate v0.1.9 is SEMANTIC_INPUT; plan v0.9.46b preimage and the pinned manifest are REVIEW_CONTEXT. Hash every additional cited source, plan, proposed HTML and previous packet before review.
